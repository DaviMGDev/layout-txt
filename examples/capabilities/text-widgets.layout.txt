# text-widgets — bare lines are text widgets (G2)
# Headings, captions, and paragraphs are STRUCTURE: presence, order, and
# label are verified. Type is "text" by convention; the label is the
# trimmed line.

Welcome to the Acme dashboard
Your workspace summary for today

# A text widget can also be written explicitly with a type:
[Explicit text widget](text)

(card)
  Q3 revenue up 12% quarter over quarter
  Driven by the new checkout flow and partner program.

# Watch out: a line starting with ( or [ parses as a WIDGET, and a line
# starting with # is a NOTE — rephrase prose that would collide:
[In progress — not "(in progress)"](text)

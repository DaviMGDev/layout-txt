## Reasoning chains
- Free-form vocabulary + verification is reconcilable: verify structure (presence/order/hierarchy/labels); vocabulary is convention with informational-only reports. Layout-first workflow is a legit reason: sketches often lead the design system.
- Space-separated variants would reintroduce a closed vocabulary through the back door (ambiguous parse: type "button primary" vs type "button" + variant) — incompatible with free-form choice. Colon is the only compatible form; separate bracket adds a third grammar shape.
- First-token rule keeps grammar at two shapes + one parse rule while preserving free-form flexibility; lenient for humans, deterministic for agents.
- Grammar now complete at ~7 rules; incompleteness-as-feature provides the escape hatch.

## Key insights
- The vocabulary decision constrains the syntax decision (free-form ⇒ colon variants).
- Two shapes ([label](type), (type)) + first-token rule + # notes + indentation covers wireframe scope.
- Verification = structural (what/where/how many), vocabulary = soft convention.
